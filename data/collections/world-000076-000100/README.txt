ISLANDS 000076–000100 — World Islands / Soft Stone

25 individual physical islands, each in North-Up and South-Up orientation.
50 PNG originals, 2048 × 2048 pixels, 300 dpi. Approximate geographic extent:
85% of the square canvas. No labels, political borders, or stepped relief.

Jan Mayen; Flores (Azores); Graciosa; Corvo; Eigg; Rùm; Gigha; La Désirade;
Marie-Galante; Carriacou; Bequia; Mocha; Robinson Crusoe; Manitoulin Island;
Vanua Balavu; Kadavu; Rarotonga; Niue; Rodrigues; Silhouette; La Digue;
Nosy Be; Fogo; Brava; Ometepe.

The coastlines use physical OpenStreetMap island ways where available,
checked against GSHHG 2.3.7 full-resolution land polygons (L1), or islands
in lakes (L3) for Manitoulin and Ometepe. Corvo additionally uses joined
OpenStreetMap natural=coastline segments, retained as raw OSM evidence. Each outline contains
its documented GeoNames reference point and passes a broad expected-area
sanity check. No administrative boundaries are used as physical silhouettes.
Reported areas are approximate polygon areas, not official cadastral land
areas. They can include inland water in the outer island polygon.

Mapped inland water comes from GSHHG L2/L4, supplemented with OpenStreetMap
water geometry where retrieved. Raw OSM responses and geometry sources are
retained here; only water polygons located within the selected island are used.
No lakes are invented. Inland-water coverage is selected, not exhaustive.
The same shoreline and water masks are used for North-Up and South-Up.

Relief uses publicly available Mapzen/AWS elevation tiles. All rendered land
pixels must have at least 99.5% elevation coverage before nearest-gap filling.
Soft Stone relief is an artistic interpretation of terrain, not an altitude
or navigation map. Light remains upper-left in both orientations. All 50
editions have individually selected palettes and unique background colors.

Country / territory, region, source IDs, approximate areas and artwork print
scales are included. Settlement labels are included only where verified;
unknown settlement data remains explicitly unverified.
