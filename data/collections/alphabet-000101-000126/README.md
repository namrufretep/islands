# A–Z Islands · 000101–000126

26 new geographic islands, one per initial A–Z; 52 independently coloured and lit North-Up/South-Up previews. Originals are 2048×2048 PNG at nominal 300 dpi. No text, labels or political boundaries are drawn into the artwork.

Physical coastline: GSHHG 2.3.7 full-resolution L1, Wessel and Smith, LGPL-3.0-or-later. Reference/name identifiers: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0. Whole-island elevation: public Mapzen elevation tile service, with tile URLs recorded per artwork. Inland waters: GSHHG L2 plus successful OpenStreetMap queries for Amorgos, Bazaruto and Chiloé; © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Other OSM water queries were unavailable or rate limited and were not treated as evidence that an island has no lakes. The mapped-water inventory is not exhaustive. Captured water geometry and source URLs are retained; raw query caches are not required to render the captured geometry.

Areas are WGS84 geodesic polygon estimates from these shorelines, not surveyed present-day land areas. Shoreline data can predate reclamation or erosion. Settlements are highest-population GeoNames cities500 points inside each polygon, not an independently exhaustive census. Four islands have no supported settlement entry from this dump; their fields remain unknown. Relief is an artistic interpretation of elevation, not a geological or topographic survey.

Rendering: locally centered azimuthal-equidistant projection; 85% longest-dimension fit, recorded metric scale; 512-pixel elevation field with independent full-resolution coastline rasterization. South-Up reverses geographic orientation and recomputes screen-relative upper-left lighting. Each edition has its own palette and background. All 52 background hex values are distinct in this batch; the batch includes dark-on-dark, pale-on-pale and complementary saturated combinations.

These are artwork previews. Registration in the app, metadata generation and launch-plan token allocation do not mint or release an NFT. The public gallery still displays confirmed minted editions; administrator view includes unreleased previews.
