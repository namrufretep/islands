# ISLANDS — BaseFriend Builder Grant Proposal (Draft)

**Project:** ISLANDS — Collect the World  
**Platform:** Farcaster Mini App on Base  
**Demo:** https://islands-kappa.vercel.app  
**Status:** Working prototype; minting, discounts, tips and trading are not live  
**Grant requested:** To be confirmed against current program rules (no award implied)

## Project summary
ISLANDS turns real-world islands into individual collectible artworks. Geographic coastline and elevation data provide the foundation for a consistent but expressive Soft Stone art direction. Collectors will explore the world through an interactive atlas and, after launch, discover and collect North-Up and South-Up island editions.

The app is designed to make BaseFriend Creator Keys useful beyond a portfolio screen: staked keys will eventually unlock verifiable mint discounts, and $CHEERS will allow people to appreciate artworks and their current owners.

## What already exists
- Farcaster Mini App foundation and wallet connection
- Interactive World Atlas, searchable Island Index and preview Gallery
- Three proposed mint modes: Discovery, Expedition and Destination
- BaseFriend market discovery and wallet holdings lookup
- Proposed Creator Key discount simulator, up to 25%
- Prototype $KEY pricing and market quote display
- Draft art studio with geography-based Bouvetøya and Sicily studies
- FAQ describing roadmap and non-live functionality

## What the grant would fund
1. **Verified Creator Key benefits:** production-ready staked-key eligibility, trustworthy pricing and wallet-specific discount calculations.
2. **Onchain collecting:** secure Base mint contract integration, edition supply controls and transparent $KEY payment flow.
3. **Owner appreciation:** $CHEERS tips linked to verified current NFT ownership and an auditable activity history.
4. **Discovery and collection:** a reliable published-edition registry, searchable gallery and user collection view.
5. **Art quality and accessibility:** reproducible geographic render pipeline, preview checks, mobile accessibility and FAQ.

## Why BaseFriend
ISLANDS offers a clear use case for staking Creator Keys and for the $KEY / $CHEERS ecosystem: collectors have a reason to discover creator markets, stake keys, return to the app, collect artworks and celebrate owners.

## Milestones (proposed, subject to scope and funding)
- **M1 — Reliable foundations:** finalize artwork review and edition registry; publish documented release status.
- **M2 — Keyholder integration:** verified stake lookups, discount logic and integration tests.
- **M3 — Collecting on Base:** mint flow, supply accounting, payment and contract security review.
- **M4 — Community engagement:** verified-owner $CHEERS appreciation and public activity metrics.

## Success measures
- Verified keyholder discount calculations and successful testnet-to-mainnet mint flow
- Number of unique collectors and released island editions
- Repeat atlas and gallery visits
- Number of verified Creator Key benefit redemptions
- Number of owner-directed $CHEERS tips, once enabled

## Risks and safeguards
No unreleased edition is offered as a live mint. Prices, staking discounts and $CHEERS payments are explicitly labeled as proposed or simulated until production integrations are verified. Geographic data and artwork previews undergo review before publication. Trading and swaps require separate implementation and security review.
