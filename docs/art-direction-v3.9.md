# ISLANDS — Art direction v3.9

## Collection-wide direction
Preserve verified geographic coastlines, terrain-guided relief, 2048 × 2048 canvas, 300 dpi target, 85% maximum island extent, and consistent upper-left screen lighting. North-Up and South-Up are separate editions. No text inside the artwork. Avoid black internal outlines, stepped relief, polygonal faceting, excessive blur and exaggerated 3D.

## Color diversity
Proposed minimum: 256 mineral-inspired palettes and 128 independent background colors (32,768 theoretical pairings), expandable over time. Include warm and cool minerals, vivid and muted hues, light and dark palettes, monochrome and unusual combinations. Backgrounds may be neutral, pastel, dark, earthy or saturated. Do not bind each island to a geographically literal color. Select compatible palette/background combinations by contrast and artistic review, not unrestricted random pairing. Avoid making all editions visually identical. The two orientations may have different palettes and backgrounds.

## Sicily v3.9 next study
Improve relief definition, matte mineral surface, subtle coastal bevel and recognizable terrain including Etna, without altering verified coastline. Retain smooth but not blurred transitions. Compare multiple palette/background pairings before approval.

## Status
Art direction proposal, not implemented in the renderer. Exact palette catalog, rarity distribution and final combinations remain to be approved. No existing artwork is automatically approved for minting.

## Expanded proposal
Eight initial color families: Mineral & Earth, Ocean & Ice, Botanical, Volcanic, Precious Stones, Electric & Experimental, Monochrome, Pastel & Porcelain. Explore matte limestone, polished stone, fine sandstone, jade, marble, obsidian and porcelain surface treatments; subtle background gradients and material textures are optional studies. Preserve geography and collection-wide relief language. All counts and treatments are proposals, not implemented or approved rarity rules.
