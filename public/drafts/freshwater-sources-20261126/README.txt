ISLANDS #000051–#000063 — Soft Stone

13 physical freshwater islands, 26 North-Up/South-Up artworks.
Olkhon, Ukerewe, Idjwi, Likoma, Zapatera, Samosir, Isle Royale, Michipicoten Island, René-Levasseur Island, Valaam, Bananal, Don Khon, Khortytsia.

2048×2048 PNG, 300 dpi. Individually selected palettes: 26 different background colors; broad directional washes and soft sculptural shadows. No political boundaries or labels in the artwork.

Geography: GSHHG 2.3.7 full-resolution L3 physical lake islands, L4 inland water, and physical OpenStreetMap place=island outlines. Water geometry uses mapped GSHHG / OSM lakes and ponds. Coastline source references, geometry and configuration are included. Relief is an artistic interpretation of Mapzen/AWS terrain, not a topographic map.

All source outlines were validated and terrain coverage checked across the full island. Inner-water coverage is selective, not exhaustive: Bananal includes selected mapped floodplain lakes; seasonal inundation varies. Unverified largest-settlement values are omitted. Artwork remains a gallery preview, separate from public mint availability.
