ISLANDS #000064–#000075 — African Islands / Soft Stone

Twelve individual physical islands, each with North-Up and South-Up artwork:
Annobón, Príncipe, Rolas, Rubane, Orango, Caravela, Ibo, Inhaca, Mohéli,
Anjouan, Kiwayu and Buvuma. Buvuma is a freshwater island in Lake Victoria.

2048 × 2048 PNG, 300 dpi, approximately 85% geographic extent. No text or
political boundaries occur in the artwork. All 24 editions have individually
selected palettes and different background colors, with restrained broad washes.

Physical shorelines: OpenStreetMap place=island polygons where available;
GSHHG 2.3.7 full-resolution physical coastlines and lake-island outlines otherwise.
Every selected island was checked against its reference point and GSHHG baseline.
The source, vertex count, polygon area and baseline overlap are recorded separately.
Some source outlines differ materially, particularly at reclaimed or tidal coasts;
these differences are retained in geography-validation.json, not averaged away.
Areas shown in the app are approximate polygon areas, not official land surveys.

The inland waters are mapped selections, not exhaustive. Additional documented
crater-lake outlines are included for Mohéli and Anjouan. The sculptural relief
uses Mapzen/AWS elevation tiles and is an artistic interpretation, not a precise
altitude or navigation map. Complete land coverage is checked for each island.
